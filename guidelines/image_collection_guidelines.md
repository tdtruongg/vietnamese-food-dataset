# Image Collection Guidelines

## Quality Standards
1. **Resolution**: Minimum resolution of 640x640 pixels.
2. **Lighting**: Clear lighting without extreme overexposure or heavy shadows.
3. **Clutter**: Avoid extreme background noise where the dish is unidentifiable.
4. **Diversity**: Maintain balanced diversity across domains:
   - Restaurant serving photos
   - Home-cooked meals
   - Street food stalls
   - Social media food blogs

## Copyright & Attribution
- All images gathered must record source URL, creator, and license status in `metadata/sources.csv`.
- Prefer open licenses (CC-BY, Public Domain) or explicit field photography.
